# HirePerfect Assessment Methodology & Scoring Standards

HirePerfect is committed to transparent, statistically sound, and defensible candidate assessment. This document details the exact standards used to calculate scores, analyze response patterns, and evaluate assessment integrity.

---

## 1. Skill Score Calculation

### Flat-Mark Objective Evaluation
All multiple-choice questions have a single verified correct answer.
- **Marks per Correct Answer:** 1.0 point
- **Negative Marking:** Disabled by default. We measure demonstrated competency rather than a candidate's appetite for risk.
- **Unanswered Questions:** 0.0 points awarded. We distinguish between questions the candidate actively skipped versus questions never reached due to time limits.

$$\text{Skill Percentage} = \text{round}\left(\frac{\text{Raw Score}}{\text{Maximum Available Points}} \times 100, 1\right)$$

---

## 2. Proficiency Bands

We map scores to standardized proficiency bands rather than issuing reductive pass/fail stamps:

- **Expert (85–100%):** Demonstrates comprehensive mastery across both fundamental and advanced topics.
- **Proficient (70–84%):** Strong working competency with minimal gaps.
- **Developing (55–69%):** Basic conceptual understanding with clear areas for targeted improvement.
- **Foundational (0–54%):** Early-stage domain knowledge requiring substantial foundational preparation.

---

## 3. Sub-Skill & Topic Breakdown

Each assessment is broken down into specific domain competencies (topics).

### Minimum-Sample Reliability Rule
To ensure candidates and recruiters never receive misleading data, we enforce a strict **minimum-sample threshold**:
- A topic must contain at least **4 questions** in the assessment to display a percentage score.
- Topics with fewer than 4 questions display raw counts (e.g., *"2 of 2 correct"*) with a clear note explaining that the sample is too small for a definitive percentage.

---

## 4. Behavioral & Timing Analysis

We analyze server-verified response timings to deliver actionable insights into candidate problem-solving:
- **Pacing & Median Duration:** Identifies standard response cadence per question.
- **Rushed Answers:** Questions answered in under 8 seconds. We separate rushed correct answers from rushed incorrect answers to distinguish between quick mastery and random guessing.
- **Answer Revisions:** Tracks how often candidates second-guess and revise answers, highlighting shifts from incorrect to correct choices.

---

## 5. GuardEye AI Integrity Protocol

HirePerfect assessments are proctored by GuardEye AI to ensure test integrity.

### The Decoupled Integrity Principle
- **No Score Deductions:** Proctoring signals never alter, lower, or adjust a candidate's skill score.
- **Objective Evidence:** Proctoring events (tab switches, webcam interruptions, multiple faces) are recorded as timestamped observational evidence for human recruiters to review.
- **Explicit Coverage Reporting:** If a camera disconnection or permission issue occurs during the test, the exact duration of the gap is disclosed on the report.
